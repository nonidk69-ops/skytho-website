The mission

01

You press SOS.

One button. Your phone sends your GPS location and the mission begins.

AMI messages you right away.

Our AI assistant finds out what happened and tells you, step by step, what to do until the drone arrives and how to use the kit. A human operator is connected the whole time.

02

The AI decides.

AMI reads the conversation, works out how serious the injury is and orders the drone to take off.

03

The drone flies itself.

It flies over the forest with no pilot and steers around obstacles on its own.

04

It finds you from above.

The camera and the on-board person detector spot you from the air. It all runs on the drone, with no internet.

05

The kit lands next to you.

The drone comes down and drops the first-aid kit beside you. An operator watches it all live, and the rescuers know exactly where you are.

The drone

01The brain

Raspberry Pi 5 + Pixhawk

The Raspberry Pi recognises people in the image and talks to the app. The Pixhawk keeps the drone stable and flies it along the route.

  • Quad-core 2.4 GHz processor
  • Pixhawk 2.4.8 flight controller
  • Everything runs on the drone, offline

02The eyes

4K camera on a gimbal

The gimbal keeps the picture steady in the wind. The YOLO model we trained recognises a person in the image, even from high above.

  • 4K video
  • YOLO detection on board
  • Live feed to the operator

03The senses

LIDAR 360° + ToF

A spinning laser measures the distance to everything around and draws a map, point by point. That's how the drone avoids trees and rocks.

  • Scans 360° on every turn
  • ToF sensor for the height above the ground
  • Sees obstacles in every direction

04Propulsion

4 brushless motors

Brushless outrunner motors. The controller adjusts their speed hundreds of times a second to keep the drone stable.

  • Outrunner motors
  • Propellers of about 10 inches
  • Two spin clockwise, two anticlockwise

05Navigation

M8N GPS

It listens to several satellite constellations and takes the drone to the exact location sent from the app.

  • GPS and GLONASS
  • Compass for heading
  • Your coordinates, from your phone

06Power

6000 mAh battery

It powers the motors, the computers and the sensors. It sits in the middle of the drone to keep the weight balanced.

  • 6000 mAh
  • Mounted at the centre of gravity
  • Quick to swap between missions

07Payload

First-aid kit

Held under the drone by a pin. Above you, a servo pulls the pin and the kit drops next to you.

  • Servo release
  • Dressings, bandage, thermal blanket
  • Lands a step away from you

The project

The app

A red button and a chat. That's all an injured person should have to use.

You open the app and press SOS. Your phone sends your GPS location and an emergency message to our server.

AMI, our AI assistant, messages you straight away. It asks what happened and where it hurts, tells you what to do until help arrives and lets you know the drone is on its way. Then a human operator takes over the conversation.

SOS button

Two display modes, so the screen stays readable in the dark.

Frequently asked questions

What a SAR drone is and how SKYTHO works, in short.

What is a SAR drone?

A SAR (search and rescue) drone looks for people who are lost or injured and helps them until rescuers arrive. SKYTHO is an autonomous SAR drone: it flies by itself to the location sent from your phone, finds you with a camera and an AI model, and drops a first-aid kit next to you.

How does SKYTHO work?

You press SOS in the app and your phone sends your GPS location. AMI, our AI assistant, finds out what happened and gives the order to take off. The drone flies with no pilot, avoids obstacles with its LIDAR, recognises you from the air with a YOLO model, comes down and releases the kit next to you. An operator follows the mission live.

Does it need the internet?

Person detection runs on the drone, offline. To stay in touch with the app and the operator, the drone uses a 4G/5G modem, with radio as a backup.

What's in the first-aid kit?

Sterile dressings, an elastic bandage and a thermal blanket. The kit is held under the drone. When the drone is above you, a servo releases it and the kit lands a step away from you.

Who built SKYTHO?

Otava Radu Ioan and Frumuzache Dorin, 8th-grade students from Galați, Romania, at the Galați County Centre of Excellence. The project won 2nd Prize at the Romanian National Scientific Creativity Olympiad 2026.
